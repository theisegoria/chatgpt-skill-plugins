# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Theisegoria's Wolfram Notebooks, MarkChart Mermaid, System Modeler Projects, and MindNode Mind Maps plugins are skills-only software packages. They do not connect to a developer-operated server, require a developer account, include developer telemetry, or send generated files to Theisegoria.

The plugins create and inspect files in locations the user or ChatGPT/Codex selects. Optional runtime validation may open or invoke locally installed desktop software, including Wolfram, MarkChart, Wolfram System Modeler, or MindNode. Those applications and the ChatGPT/Codex product may process data under their own privacy policies and settings.

The plugins do not intentionally collect personal information. Generated artifacts can contain information supplied by the user, so users should choose storage and sharing locations appropriate to that information.

Privacy questions may be raised through the [public support tracker](https://github.com/theisegoria/chatgpt-skill-plugins/issues).
